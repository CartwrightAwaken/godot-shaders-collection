## 🌟 About

**Godot Shaders Collection** is a **handpicked library of 60+ shaders** built specifically for **Godot 4**. Each shader is optimized, well-commented, and ready to drop into your project — from photorealistic water and volumetric fog to stylized toon outlines and retro CRT effects.

No more searching through random forum posts or adapting Shadertoy snippets. Everything here is **Godot-native**, tested, and documented.

> 💡 **Perfect for:** Indie game developers, Godot learners, VFX artists, and anyone who wants to add visual polish without writing shader code from scratch.
